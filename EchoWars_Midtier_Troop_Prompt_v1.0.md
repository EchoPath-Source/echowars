- Real-time 1v1, 3-minute matches. Resonance regenerates 1/sec, max 10. Cards cost 2–8.

- Abilities must be expressible in real time: auras, on-hit, on-death, zones, timed effects. No dice rolls, no Will Checks, no turn-based triggers — translate the fiction into continuous mechanics.
