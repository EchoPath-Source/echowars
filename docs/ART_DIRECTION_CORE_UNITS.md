# Echo Wars Core Unit Anti-Drift Sheet — Wave 1 & Wave 2

## Purpose
Use this sheet to keep core-unit renders aligned across factions, avoid accidental legendary/boss drift, and preserve gameplay readability for mobile, card art, and future 3D conversion.

## Global rules
- Core units must feel repeatable, field-deployable, and readable at thumbnail size.
- Avoid giant halos, oversized relics, excessive ornament, boss-tier VFX, and main-character poses.
- Every unit should look like it was caught in the middle of doing its job.
- Faction identity should read in 2 seconds.

## Wave 1 baselines
### Wandbearer of Echo — Awakened Ones
- Young field caster, hooded, wand only.
- Calm blue resonance glow, simple support silhouette.
- Not a saint, not a priest-king, not a chosen hero.

### Echo Leech — Loopbound
- Three-body low swarm unit.
- Greyhound-leech blend with recursive exhaustion.
- Patient, practiced drain predators; elegant wrongness, not gore.

### Collapse Scribe — Architects of Collapse
- Senior architect-scholar, codex carrier, stillness as authority.
- Light-value architect palette with warm ochre/parchment and dark structure.
- Geometric, procedural, observational.

### Flamebearer — Forgotten Flame
- Hooded scarred votary, cloth-first silhouette.
- Curved ember blade, old burn damage, forward commitment.
- Grief carried into motion, not rage or spectacle.

## Wave 2 baselines
### Veil Walker — Awakened Ones
- Threshold specialist; never fully settled in one lane.
- Hood up, face partly shadowed, hair moving in unstable air.
- Small orb as resonance compass, not a weapon.
- Costume split visible on the garment itself: one side slightly darker, one side lighter.

### Cycle Priest — Loopbound
- Recursive ritualist tier between Echo Leech and Distortion Walker.
- Vertical support/control silhouette.
- Should feel worn-through by repetition, not exalted.

### Distortion Walker — Loopbound
- Humanoid flanker whose body and gear are slightly offset.
- Soldier silhouette first, distortion second.
- Controlled wrongness, not chaos; clean fractures, displaced copies, calm visor read.

### Measure Guard — Architects of Collapse
- Formation specialist; armor is a geometric proof.
- Heavy, still, professional, faceted enclosed helmet.
- Spear at rest, optional tower shield at mathematically optimal angle.

### Ashbound Votary — Forgotten Flame
- Open offering rather than attack.
- Almost entirely cloth, ember mark in chest, palms open with contained embers.
- Stillness radiates outward; sacred transaction, not explosive fire magic.

## Faction read rules
### Awakened Ones
- Upright, luminous, measured, clean.
- Simple ritual tools, restrained blue-white resonance, sacred geometry used quietly.

### Loopbound
- Low or wrong, patient, recursive, inward pressure.
- Elegant corruption, offset memory, practiced erosion.

### Architects of Collapse
- Still, geometric, procedural, contained authority.
- Lighter value family, parchment/ochre/brass over graphite structure.

### Forgotten Flame
- Forward or open, heat from within, scarred return.
- Cloth and ash first; one ember point is stronger than many flames.

## Quick drift check
- Does this look like a troop class rather than a named champion?
- Is there one primary read instead of five competing reads?
- Can this silhouette survive card-size reduction?
- Would three copies on a battlefield still make sense?
- Did the render accidentally become cooler than a future elite or Echoform?
