# Echo Wars Faction Canon — Working Sync

_Source references: `Echo_Wars_Faction_Canon_Lore_Sync_Dossier.docx`, `Echo_Wars_Core_Thread_Dossier.docx`_

## Shared world canon
Echo Wars is a resonance-first conflict setting in **Nytherra**. Victory is not defined only by destruction, but by alignment, remembrance, erosion, containment, sacrifice, and return.

The four founding factions are:
- The Awakened Ones
- The Loopbound
- The Architects of Collapse
- The Forgotten Flame

These same factions should remain visually and mechanically legible across the mobile game, the tabletop game, future cards, 3D models, and promotional material.

## Faction identity summary
### The Awakened Ones
**Core law:** remembrance as living order.

**Emotional read:** spiritual steadiness, witness, sacred purpose, calm inevitability.

**Visual canon:** hooded or cloaked figures, layered robes over structured ritual armor, restrained blue-white resonance, mirror/witness motifs, stable sacred geometry.

**Anti-drift:**
- Do not turn them into generic bright-gold paladins.
- Do not over-neon the faction into cyan sci-fantasy.
- Calm does not mean passive or weak.

### The Loopbound
**Core law:** erosion through repetition.

**Emotional read:** recursive depletion, mimicry, false return, patient wrongness.

**Visual canon:** low pressure, offset anatomy, ritual decay, swarm logic, memory drain, violet and bruised-indigo corruption.

**Anti-drift:**
- Do not default them into gore monsters or rage demons.
- Wrongness should feel refined, practiced, and recursive.
- Their pressure is psychological and spatial, not only violent.

### The Architects of Collapse
**Core law:** structure, measure, inevitability through design.

**Emotional read:** certainty, stillness, procedural control, containment.

**Visual canon:** geometric logic, controlled coat/armor silhouettes, faceted design, lighter parchment/ochre/brass value family over graphite structure.

**Anti-drift:**
- Do not make them holy knights.
- Do not over-glow the triangles.
- They are surveyors, judges, and formation specialists more than wizards.

### The Forgotten Flame
**Core law:** return through ruin.

**Emotional read:** scarred vow, committed cost, grief carried forward, embered resolve.

**Visual canon:** scorched cloth, repaired gear, internal heat, ash and ember, open offering or forward commitment.

**Anti-drift:**
- Do not make them lava bosses or rage berserkers.
- One ember point is stronger than many flames.
- Burn history matters more than spectacle.
