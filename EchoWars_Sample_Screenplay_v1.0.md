**FADE IN:**

**SCENE 1 — VERSUS**

*Black. A low drone, felt more than heard.*

*Stage-left: a sigil ignites in burning BLUE — the Awakened Ones. Beneath it, the tagline fades in: "We remember. Therefore we remain." A whisper of glass chimes.*

*Stage-right: a sigil tears open in deep PURPLE — the Loopbound. "What repeats, erodes." The audio folds back on itself, a loop degrading as it cycles.*

*Hold three beats. The sigils flare —*

**SCENE 2 — THE ARENA**

*Overhead. The chromosome: two lanes of dark glass crossing at the center, the Veiled lane breathing faint mist on the left, the Field lane stark on the right. At the crossing, the Center Node — a crystal structure, shimmering white, unclaimed.*

*Below the arena, two hands of five cards, face-down to us, glowing faintly at the edges — blue on one side, purple on the other. Beneath each hand, a Resonance bar, empty, beginning to fill with light. 3:00 on the clock.*

*The drone rises. Chimes and degraded loops braid underneath it.*

**SCENE 3 — FIRST DEPLOYMENT (Awakened)**

*The blue player drags a card forward. On release, the card BURNS — blue-white flame eats it in under a second.*

*A vertical tear rips open above the Field lane, blazing blue. Through it steps a WANDBEARER OF ECHO, staff grounding, and as its feet touch the glass, a soft ring of blue light spreads — the Resonance Pulse, breathing.*

*The purple player answers: a card burns violet. The tear opens over the Veiled lane — and THREE ECHO LEECHES spill through, skittering, each trailing a thread of purple static.*

**SCENE 4 — FIRST BLOOD**

*The leeches reach the Wandbearer. They tear at it — 40, 40, 40 — and the Wandbearer's light gutters. Below 20%. It enters DRIFT: its blue aura stutters, flickers, comes loose at the edges.*

*A TUNING FORK strikes — one pure, cold note that seems to come from inside the player's skull.*

*The Wandbearer SHUDDERS, vibrating between two frequencies. Its blue drains downward like liquid light into the glass — and purple rises up through it from below. Where an Awakened stood, a Loopbound instrument now stands, wreathed in violet, a faint blue after-image clinging to it like a ghost.*

*It turns. Raises the staff. Fires on its former allies.*

*Fifteen seconds. Then it dissolves — it was never truly theirs.*

**SCENE 5 — THE NODE**

*While the flanks burn, an Awakened MIRROR SENTINEL has walked the center. It plants itself on the Node — and the crystal shudders, then kindles BLUE, shimmering, held.*

*Somewhere in the dark, the forge-fire of the Awakened burns a little brighter.*

**FADE OUT.**
